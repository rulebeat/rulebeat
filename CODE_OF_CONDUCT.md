# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want RuleBeat's issues, discussions, and pull requests to be a place where anyone can report a
bug, ask a question, or propose a change without being made to feel unwelcome for it. This applies
regardless of experience level, background, or how the request is phrased.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful in disagreement. Critique the idea or the code, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Most confusing questions are genuine, not trolling.
- Accept that maintainer time is limited (see [`SUPPORT.md`](SUPPORT.md)); a slow response isn't a
  dismissal.
- Give and receive feedback that focuses on what's actually wrong and what would fix it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, or private contact made
  through the project.
- Publishing someone else's private information without consent.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately derailing a thread away from its purpose.

## Scope

This applies to every space the project runs directly: the GitHub repository (issues, discussions,
pull requests, commit comments) and any direct contact made through it. It does not extend to
unrelated platforms just because someone mentions RuleBeat there.

## Enforcement

Report a violation through the private contact in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) rather than in a
public issue. Include what happened and, if possible, a link. This is a solo-maintained project
(see [`SUPPORT.md`](SUPPORT.md) for what response times to expect), but reports are read and acted
on: that can mean a warning, editing or removing content, or blocking someone from the repository,
depending on severity and whether it's a repeat.
